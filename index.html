01 / The benchmark

Business workflows with state.

Each task binds a user goal and an initial world to a task-specific executable contract. Trials run against synthetic records and deterministic tools, without a live CRM backend.

  1. 1

    Reset the business world

    Start with fresh records, domain policies, and the task’s declared initial state.

  2. 2

    Let the interaction unfold

    An on-policy simulated user responds to the agent’s visible replies. The reference scaffold routes each turn to scoped tools.

  3. 3

    Check the executed contract

    Grade the recorded dialogue, tool trace, and final state. Strict success requires every task-declared check to hold.

Tasks and their contracts are separate from the reference agent. Latency and cost are reported alongside correctness.

How to read these scores

Strict success is the conjunction of the checks declared by each task. It measures the complete evaluated agent system.

Three-round mean averages the three round-level success rates. Sample standard deviation is in percentage points. All-three success is the percentage of tasks that succeed in all three executions, restricted to tasks eligible for evaluation in every round.

Success rates use tasks with an eligible verdict; unavailable verdicts are excluded.
